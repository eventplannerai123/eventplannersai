# The tracker shot

`Vendor Payment Tracker — May 15, 2027 wedding`, Google Sheet
`1P6zCWC18kY-SC2oocX7xzZcEMF0CXxEg3f6qDqdiOqo`, in Drive `November 5`. A copy of
the product tracker, filled here with the 17 payments from the eight
"Reel — vendor payments" emails, so she only has to open it and record.

**Why it was filled from here and not on camera.** The Thu 5 block says to paste
the table into the tracker during the recording. Pasting a 15-column table into
Sheets on a phone lands as one squashed column as often as not, and she films on
the phone because the cut is vertical. So the paste happened here.

## What the sheet computes, and what it proves

| | |

| Due in the next 30 days | $16,530 |

**These are the correct figures, and the first take's answer was not.** That take
put a card on screen reading $59,180 still unpaid and $72,480 scheduled —
$13,100 over on both, about the paid deposits counted a second time. The
tracker's own formulas and an independent sum of the eight emails both give
$46,080 and $59,380. Her Prompt 1 now ends "Don't add up totals; the tracker
does the math."

## THERE IS NO RED, AND THE SCRIPT HAD TO CHANGE

The brief asks for "the flags turning red and amber". The conditional formatting
only colours three states: `Paid` green, `Due this week` amber, `Overdue` red.
On Nov 5 the nearest payment is Petal Press on Nov 14 — nine days out — so
nothing is overdue and nothing is due this week. The sheet shows five green rows
and twelve uncoloured ones.

So the voiceover line "the ones coming up in the next thirty days turn red"
would have described something not on screen. It now reads "get flagged", which
is what the Flag column actually says.

**The payoff shot is the Summary tab, not the colours.** Six numbers, all
correct, and the contrast with the first take's card is the whole point of the
piece.
