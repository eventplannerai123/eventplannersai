# Why this caption carries no `AI Prompt:` block

Every other caption ends with the prompt, so the post stands on its own without
the video. **This one must not**, and the guide's own Thu 5 caption leaves it
out: the six prompts *are* the $24 product. Pasting Prompt 1 under a launch
Reel gives away the thing the Reel exists to sell, and Prompt 1 is the one
buyers run first.

So the caption names what the product does and stops. The CTA is the guide's:
**Comment BUDGET** — reinstated for this launch after being retired for nine
failed keywords, because this time a ManyChat auto-reply sends the Gumroad link
back, which none of those nine ever had.

**The TikTok caption differs by one line**, as the Thu 5 block requires: the
CTA points at Instagram ("Comment BUDGET on my Instagram"), because the
auto-reply only exists there.
