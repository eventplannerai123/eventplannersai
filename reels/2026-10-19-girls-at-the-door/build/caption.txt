A gossip blog once wrote that the girls at the door were “in way over their heads.” I was one of the girls at the door.

It was my first big event, almost 20 years ago. A freezing night, a client holiday party, and a guest list that fell apart: the invitation had been forwarded around, people RSVP’d who were never invited, and guests arrived with half their office. It took four of my bosses at the door to sort it out.

Today I’d catch it before anyone got in line. Paste your invite list and your RSVP list into AI and ask: “Match these two lists. Flag every RSVP that wasn’t on the invite list, every duplicate, and every plus-one over the limit. Then give me a door list sorted by company, with the person who approved each guest.”
