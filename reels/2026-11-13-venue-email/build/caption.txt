This email got $10,000 off the venue quote.

$45,000 for a Saturday. The small print said Sunday–Thursday rates and multi-year agreements were available on request, and we run this event every year — so the email asks for a Wednesday and a two-year commitment, and for the rate on each. Last time I sent one like it, $45,000 came back as $35,000. It even added a negotiation tip: don't name a target price, let them come back with their best weekday and multi-year numbers first. One thing I'd add — it offers the two years without asking them to lock the rate in return.
