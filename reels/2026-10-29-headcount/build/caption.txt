This is why I never ask AI for food quantities with just a headcount.

A 150-person reception might be:
two hours, straight from the office
no dinner after
a room full of people who skipped lunch

Another 150-person reception might be:
45 minutes before a seated dinner
guests arriving in waves
half of them still stuck in a session

Same headcount. Completely different order.

Give ChatGPT a number and it guesses. Give it the timing, what comes next and who's in the room, and it does the maths properly.
