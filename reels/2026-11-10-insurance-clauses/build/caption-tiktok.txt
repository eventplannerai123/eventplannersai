The contract asks for $2 million in liquor liability. The policy has $1 million.

Four requirements don't match: the liquor limit, the additional insured box, the waiver of subrogation, and the dates. The one it ranked first wasn't the limit — the certificate expires March 31 and the event is April 17. One thing I'd change: it tells you to raise your own liquor limit, and usually the caterer holds the license, so the fix is their certificate naming the venue, not a bigger policy of yours. Confirm every fix with your broker and your legal or risk team before you sign.
