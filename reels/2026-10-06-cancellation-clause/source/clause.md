ARTICLE 9 — CANCELLATION AND TERMINATION

9.1 Deposit. A non-refundable deposit of twenty-five percent (25%) of the
Estimated Total is due upon execution and shall be retained by the Venue in all
circumstances. The deposit is not applied against any liquidated damages
assessed under Section 9.2.

9.2 Cancellation by Client. Should the Client cancel, the Client shall pay the
Venue liquidated damages according to the date written notice is received at
the Venue's offices:

  180 days or more prior to the Event Date .......... 25% of the Estimated Total
  179 to 90 days prior .............................. 50% of the Estimated Total
  89 to 60 days prior ............................... 75% of the Estimated Total
  59 days or fewer prior ........................... 100% of the Estimated Total

9.3 Estimated Total. For purposes of Section 9.2, "Estimated Total" means the
contracted Food and Beverage Minimum, contracted room rental, the service
charge of twenty-two percent (22%) and all applicable taxes, whether or not
such amounts have been incurred.

9.4 Notice. Notice is effective on the date of receipt and not the date of
sending. Notice received after 5:00 p.m. local time shall be deemed received on
the following business day.

9.5 Rebooking. Should the Client rebook a comparable event at the Venue within
twelve (12) months of the cancelled Event Date, fifty percent (50%) of the
liquidated damages paid may be applied as a credit toward the rebooked event.
Credit is non-transferable and expires if the rebooked event is cancelled or
postponed.

9.6 Force Majeure. Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform due to
causes beyond its reasonable control. Economic hardship, reduced attendance,
loss of funding, and inability to obtain a permit shall not constitute force
majeure.
