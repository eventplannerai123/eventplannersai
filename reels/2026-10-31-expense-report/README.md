# Expense report Reel — Sat 31 Oct

Two cuts, 25.2s, 1080x1920, Eryn. Caption `build/caption.txt`, hash `e1cd8885`.

## What she recorded is not what the brief describes, and it is better

The brief specs pasting twelve Chicago receipts into the chat and lists what the
answer should catch: a duplicate $64.10 dinner, four receipts with no date,
total $1,653.75.

She did something else. She pointed ChatGPT at her **Gmail**, uploaded photos of
three paper receipts and a screenshot of a card statement for a receipt she had
lost, attached her **own expense template**, and asked for it back as a filled
Excel file with the layout, columns, formulas and drop-downs untouched.

So the brief's numbers do not apply: nothing is undated here, and the catch is a
duplicate **$186.40** Copper Fork dinner plus one **$22.64** charge that has a
card statement and no itemised receipt. The voiceover, the takeaway overlay and
the caption are written around the Denver run — which is the guide's own
instruction, not a departure from it: *"CC rewrites the last voiceover line, the
takeaway overlay and the caption's second paragraph around what she actually
caught, after the recording."*

**The takeaway changed with it.** The brief's "Paste the mess. Ask it to flag
duplicates before it totals." was written for a demo where she pastes. She
doesn't. It reads **"Point it at your inbox. Make it flag duplicates before it
totals."**

## The twist line is hers, chosen from three

She asked for options and picked:

> The one thing I'd change: it flagged a twenty-two dollar card-statement meal
> as no receipt. For that amount a statement is usually fine.

Recorded because the guide's twist is defined as *"the one line Sahiba would
still change"* — it has to be her judgement, not a plausible invention. Three
candidates went over, she chose, and the chosen one is in the cut verbatim.

## Three things the footage forced

**The crop is y-offset 440, not CLAUDE.md's 478.** At 478 the top ~6px of the
attachment thumbnails goes, and those thumbnails are the point of the opening
frame: they are what tells you she uploaded a template and photographed
receipts. 440 still clears the status bar and the red recording dot.

**The hook moved to y=1180.** The opening frame is prompt bubble top to bottom —
there is no empty space. At y=250 the card covered *"Search my Gmail for every
receipt from my work trip to Denver"*, which is the line the opening exists to
show. Low puts it over *"Fill it in, one row per receipt"*, which nobody needs
to read to understand the shot.

**Her clip ends on her own name.** At about 12s she exits the preview into the
Files app and *"Sahiba Anand Paint...ume"* is legible in the recents list. The
beat stops at 10.8s. Check the tail of every clip, not just the part filmed on
purpose.

## The summary block is deliberately out of frame

The phone's file preview does not evaluate formulas, so every category subtotal
and the TOTAL read **$0.00** on screen. The band is cropped above it and nothing
in the script claims a total. A Reel about getting an expense report right
cannot show a $0.00 total.

Measured, the table is a 419px band in a 2622px frame — 2.9:1 — so filling a
0.5625 frame from it is impossible. It is cropped to the payoff columns
(Receipt source, Flag, Notes), upscaled 1.49x, and sat on the brand lavender.
