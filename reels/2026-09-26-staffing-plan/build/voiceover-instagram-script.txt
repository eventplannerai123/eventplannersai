Six sessions across three rooms, two hundred attendees, and four staff plus me. The question isn't whether we're short — it's when. So instead of working it out on a notepad the night before, I asked for a staffing plan in fifteen-minute blocks: registration, room turns, AV checks, breaks. And the part I actually needed — it flagged every block where we don't have enough people. It came back as a grid. Every fifteen minutes, who's on registration, who's turning rooms, who's on AV, who's actually getting a break. And the blocks where four people can't be in three places at once are right there in front of me — a week out, instead of at ten fifteen on the day.
