What it is

The Event Planners AI is an assistant for event planners and event businesses. It helps prepare event content — highlight clips, recaps, promotional posts — and publishes that content to the planner's own connected social media accounts, including TikTok.

How it works

1. You connect your account

You authorize The Event Planners AI to access your TikTok account through TikTok's official login flow. You choose which permissions to grant, and TikTok shows you exactly what is being requested before you approve anything.

2. You prepare content

You supply the video and the details — caption, privacy setting, and when it should go out. Nothing is generated and posted without your involvement.

3. It publishes on your behalf

The service uploads your video to your TikTok account, either as a draft for you to finish in the TikTok app or as a direct post, depending on what you choose.

What we access on TikTok

  • Basic profile information — your display name, avatar, and account identifier, so the service can show you which account it is connected to.
  • Content upload and posting — permission to place the videos you provide onto your own account.

We do not read your private messages, and we do not access accounts other than the ones you explicitly connect. You can disconnect at any time from your TikTok settings, and revoking access there immediately ends our ability to act on your account.
