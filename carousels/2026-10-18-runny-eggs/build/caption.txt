Nobody complains to the planner about breakfast. They just remember it was bad.

Runny eggs are the worst thing you can put in front of a conference room. Breakfast is the first thing every attendee forms an opinion about, and scrambled eggs are the dish least able to survive a long hold.

The prompt:
"Here's the breakfast BEO for a 400-person conference: [paste]. Service runs 7:30 to 9am from chafing dishes. Tell me which hot items won't hold for the full 90 minutes, how often each one needs refreshing, and what to swap in for anything that won't survive the hold. Then write the line I send the catering manager."

Save this for your next breakfast BEO.
