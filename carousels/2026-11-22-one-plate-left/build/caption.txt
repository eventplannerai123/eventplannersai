Every event needs a food and beverage captain: one person who walks the stations, calls the kitchen at half full instead of empty, and knows how long every refresh takes.

The prompt:
"Here’s the BEO and the run of show for a 300-person evening reception: [paste]. Build a food and beverage captain sheet: every station and bar, when it opens and closes, the refresh trigger (call the kitchen at half full, not empty), how long each refresh takes, the kitchen and banquet captain contacts, and a walk schedule every 15 minutes during the busiest hour. Flag any station where the refresh takes longer than the gap between guest waves."

Send this to the planner who’s also been the one refilling the crudités.
