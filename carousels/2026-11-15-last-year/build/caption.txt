Last year is data, not a template.

Stop saying "that's how we did it last year." The post-event survey already told you what to change — most of us file it and copy the agenda anyway.

Three changes it found in last year's documents: cut the 90-minute breakouts to 60 (68% said sessions ran too long), move the closing keynote off the end of day two (41% left before it), and drop the $9,800 event app that scored 2.9 out of 5 to fix the lunch line instead (52% said it was too slow).

The prompt:
"Here are last year's post-event survey results, the agenda and the final budget for our annual conference: [paste]. Tell me what to change, what to cut, and whether a different agenda flow would work better, based only on what's in these documents. Quote the survey line behind each suggestion."
