## The slide-4 type was too small on the first build

The engine is the Wed 11 one, which caps an items slide's headline at 78px
because its three items were one short line each. These carry a whole sentence
of evidence, and the item type is derived as a fraction of the headline — so
the cap pushed the evidence down to 24px while the other four slides ran at
98–100. Every slide now starts at 100 and the fit decides; slide 4 settled at
90px with the evidence at 30px.

Caught by looking at the slides, which is the only thing that catches it.
