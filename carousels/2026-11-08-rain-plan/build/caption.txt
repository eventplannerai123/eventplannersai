Everyone has a rain plan. Almost nobody has priced one.

This one added $12,880: a tent, a floor, walls, lights, a generator and the crew to put it up. If the event is outdoors, the indoor version gets priced in pre-planning, not discovered the week of.

The prompt:
"Here's the budget for an outdoor garden reception for 150 guests and the tent company's quote for the rain plan [upload]. Put the two plans side by side: every line that changes, what it adds or replaces, the total for each plan and the difference. Then tell me the date I have to make the rain call by, and what it costs if I hold the tent and don't use it."

Save this for your next outdoor event, and send it to the planner who's already watching the forecast.
