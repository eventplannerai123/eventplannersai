Don't order "sandwiches." Order a spec the kitchen can't misread.

Every conference has the dry sandwich lunch. Filling that stops an inch from the edge, bread that's been sitting since 9am. Nobody complains to the planner. They just remember the food was bad.

AI Prompt: "I'm ordering a boxed sandwich lunch for 250 conference attendees, served 12:15 to 1pm after a full morning of sessions. Here's the caterer's menu description: [paste]. Rewrite it as a spec the kitchen can't misread: filling to the edges, assembled as close to service as possible, a spread or slaw on every sandwich, vegetarian and gluten-free counts, and a backup hot option if the sandwiches arrive dry. Then write the two-line note I send the catering manager."

Save this for your next catering order.
