## The crop, which is the only change made here

| | |
| --- | --- |
| `source.png` | 1086x1448, ratio **0.75** — as she sent it |
| `future-of-ai-instagram.jpg` | 1080x1350, ratio **0.8000** — what posts |

Instagram's feed accepts 0.8 (4:5) to 1.91:1, so **0.75 is outside it** and
Instagram would have cropped the photo itself, with no say over what it took.
Cropping here keeps that choice: the 90 rows came **entirely off the bottom**,
which is audience backs, so the "THE FUTURE OF AI" headline and the full stage
survive untouched. A centred crop would also have cleared the headline — it
sits around y=210-290 — but there was no reason to spend any of the margin on
the top.

Converted to JPEG in the same pass: the Instagram API takes JPEG, and the
upload was a PNG.

The three panelists' faces were already blurred and the series name already
removed when she sent it, edited in ChatGPT on 2026-09-30. Nothing here
re-edited the image beyond the crop and the format.
