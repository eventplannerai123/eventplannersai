Event planners never actually get to go to the event.

You've been to hundreds of weddings, galas and launches. You've eaten at maybe four of them, standing up, behind a curtain, in under three minutes.

You hear the toast from the hallway. You see the first dance through a door. You watch the room light up while checking what comes next on the timeline.

And still, there's nothing like the moment the doors open and it all works. That part of the party is yours.

Be honest: when did you last sit down at your own event?
