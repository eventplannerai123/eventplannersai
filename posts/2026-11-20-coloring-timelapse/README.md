# Fri 20 Nov — the coloring timelapse (TikTok about 3pm, and her Story)

One page coloured start to finish, from `IMG_0430.MOV` — 101.8s, native
portrait 1080x1920, filmed Thu 19.

**She filmed it from the table, not overhead.** On Nov 18 she said she had no
way to film from up top, and the propped angle reads better anyway: the hand
moving is what makes a timelapse work. Phone against something about a foot
back, page filling the frame, hand entering from the side.

Trimmed **2.50–99.70**: she leans in to start the recording for the first two
and a half seconds and reaches to stop it at 100.1. 97.2 seconds at **6.075x**
gives exactly **16.00s**, inside the 15–20 second target.

**Her raw clip is not in this repo** — the repo is public. It lives in Drive
`November 20` (`13KeZV3qJI5EPl1QdT5ZMXQuNOgyAATmW`); `render.py` reads a
scratch copy.

## No voiceover — her instruction, Nov 19

this condition. One band and nothing else; the caption does the rest.

## The bed

Celesta and pizzicato strings over ukulele, `eleven_music_v2_5`, upbeat per
her Nov 10 instruction, and **its own generation** — nothing shared with
Friday's marimba planner-feelings bed, because two posts on one day never
share a track.

Instrumental proved the usual two ways: no formant movement in the
spectrogram, and Scribe returned an empty string. Trim 0–16s, 0.8s fade in,
1.2s fade out, **+2.15 dB** into the limiter for **-13.99 LUFS** on the export.

With the voice gone the bed is the whole soundtrack, which is also why it
cannot go out silent — her Nov 10 rule, in four words: "dont go out silent".
