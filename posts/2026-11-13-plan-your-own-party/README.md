## The words are the guide's

Headline, three paragraphs and the closing question were written into the
Fri 13 block, so nothing here is composed. `check()` asserts three paragraphs
and no AI demo or sales line — this format is about what the job feels like,
and the moment it carries a prompt or a product it stops being that.

## It is the inverse of the carousel brand look, deliberately

Deep purple #3C3489 ground, headline in Poppins SemiBold white, body in
Poppins Regular lavender #CECBF6, the handle small at the bottom. No numbered
badge, no EP wordmark. The guide specifies this inversion for the format.

The headline sets at 96px, the largest the layout allows, and the whole block
is centred in the band between y=118 and y=1788 so nothing lands in the top
12% or under the handle.

## The bed is the first upbeat one

Her instruction on Nov 10, after hearing the Wed 11 pair: *"moving forward
lets to more upbeat instrumentals"*. Those two were deliberately soft, because
the guide's format line says "soft royalty-free music" and the brief was to
sit behind text. She heard them and wanted more energy, so her words govern.

This one is plucked acoustic guitar and marimba over electric piano with a
light shaker pulse, major key, steady — brighter and with movement, but still
even enough not to fight the reading.

**One bed per post per day.** Friday's other piece is the venue-email Reel,
which carries an Eryn voiceover and no music at all, so there is nothing here
to clash with — but the rule stands either way after Wed 11, when both Reels
went out of here on one track and she spotted it: *"are they both the same?"*

Trimmed from **0.0s**, not 10.0s as the Wed 11 one was: the spectrogram shows
this generation starting on the full texture, with no ambient intro to fall
into under the 0.8s fade. Faded 0.8s in and 1.2s out, then **+3.4 dB** through
`alimiter=limit=0.841:level=0` to measure **-14.0 LUFS, -1.4 dBTP** on the
finished export.

**Nobody in this session can hear it**, so it is proved instrumental rather
than assumed, the same two ways as the earlier beds: a spectrogram read for
the formant movement a sung vowel makes, and Scribe, which transcribes an
instrumental to an empty string. The generation's own settings are not the
check — they can report "Instrumental: False" even when the prompt asked for
none. What neither check settles is whether it *sounds* good.
