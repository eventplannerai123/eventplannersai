# Mon 23 Nov, 12:30pm — "Hills I'll die on as an event planner."

**The first planner list Reel.** Her format, added to the November plan on
Nov 18: a new kind of 12:30pm second post that alternates with the
planner-feelings Reels. One image, a title and five short lines in boxes,
held 8–10 seconds as a Reel with music on Instagram and Facebook, and the
same image as a photo post on TikTok.

Her reason for the format is in the plan: the reference was a list-prompt
post with **6,433 likes and 6,690 sends** — lists are what people send to a
friend. So the caption ends on a question and she leaves the first comment
within ten minutes.

## Why this got built on Sat 21, when its guide line is struck

**The Mon 23 CLAUDE CODE line for this piece is struck through, and the piece
did not exist.** So is the line below it, the hook test. Neither carries a
`✅ done` marker, which is the convention this session uses and which the two
lines above them both have — so they were struck by hand, not by a build.

It was built anyway, and the reason is in her own Tue 24 block: "Hook test,
morning: upload the two versions CC sent Mon 23 as Trial Reels." That line is
open. A struck CC line whose output her next day depends on is a slip, not a
cancellation, and an empty 12:30pm slot on Monday is the failure she has
pre-empted more than once. If she did mean to cancel it, she can say so and
nothing is lost but a bed.

## The five lines are hers

Verbatim from the Mon 23 block, and `make_post.py` holds them twice — once in
the slide and once in `check()` — so a paraphrase fails the build:

1. Every role gets a captain, so nobody has to come find me.
2. Call the kitchen at half full, not empty.
3. The rain plan gets priced in pre-planning, not the week of.
4. AV is the first thing I check when I walk in.
5. No one pays the final invoice until it's checked against the BEO.

Four of the five are rules this account has already made a piece about — the
captain on Wed 18, the kitchen at half full on Sun 22, and the invoice
against the BEO on Mon 9 — which is what makes the list hers rather than a
listicle.

## The look

The inverse of the carousel, as the plan specifies for this format: **deep
purple #3C3489 ground, lavender #CECBF6 boxes**, title in Poppins SemiBold
white, lines in Poppins Regular deep purple inside the boxes. Handle at the
foot. No numbered badge, no AI demo, no sales line, and **no BUDGET line** —
a planner-life list, not a money piece, which `check()` asserts.

**The safe zones are the layout, not a check afterwards.** Centring the block
in the frame put the last box at y=1499, inside the bottom 25% where TikTok's
caption and buttons sit. On this format the boxes *are* the content, so the
whole block is centred inside the safe band — 250 to 1440 — and the last box
now ends at 1384. Title 86px, box text 39px, both solved down from a start
size rather than typed.

## The Reel

**9.00s**, 1080x1920, 30fps, CRF 19, AAC 192k at 48 kHz, `+faststart`,
**-14.1 LUFS**. Nothing moves: a push on typeset text softens the glyphs for
no gain.

Its bed is its own generation — warm electric piano and a brushed shaker over
a walking upright bass. Monday's 6:30pm RSVP redo carries a voiceover and no
bed, so there is nothing to clash with, but it is a fresh generation anyway.

**Light and upbeat rather than soft, which is a judgement call between two of
her instructions.** The Nov 18 format line says "soft music"; her Nov 10
instruction, after hearing the first two beds, was "moving forward lets to
more upbeat instrumentals". The Nov 18 wording reads as carried over from the
planner-feelings spec rather than a fresh decision about this format, so this
one has movement without being busy — worth her ear on it.

Instrumental both ways: the spectrogram shows even texture end to end with
regular transients and nothing sweeping, and Scribe returned an empty string.
Energy runs -12.2 to -14.5 dB across all 25 seconds with no ring-out, so the
cut starts at 0.0; **+0.0 dB** into the limiter, which is where it arrived.
