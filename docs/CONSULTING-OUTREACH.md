## How to check it

Two Gmail searches, both from this session:

- **Replies** — search the subject line and count threads whose
  `messageCount` is greater than 1. A thread at 1 is her own sent message with
  nothing back.
- **Bounces** — `from:mailer-daemon OR from:postmaster OR subject:"Undelivered"
  OR subject:"Delivery Status Notification" OR subject:"Address not found"`,
  dated after the send. An empty result means every address took delivery;
  it does **not** mean anyone read it.
