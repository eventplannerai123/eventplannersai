# TikTok timing test, week 1 (Nov 2–7) — what actually happened

Her plan, approved Oct 31: three evening slots (Mon 2, Thu 5, Fri 6) against
three morning ones (Tue 3, Wed 4, Sat 7), decided at the Sun 8 recap.

**The arms are not what the plan says.** She schedules TikTok by hand, and
the posting times pulled from `TIKTOK_LIST_VIDEOS` on Sat 7 are:

| Day | Planned | Actually went up | Views at Sat 7 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mon 2 — the fees nobody quotes | evening | **8:30pm** ✓ | 173 |
| Tue 3 — deposit tracker | morning | **8:30pm** ✗ | 130 |
| Wed 4 — contingency carousel | morning | **9:00am** ✓ | 216 |
| Thu 5 — launch Reel | evening | **9:10am** ✗ | 165 |
| Fri 6 — three quotes | evening | **8:45am** ✗ | 188 |
| Sat 7 — walkie-talkie image | morning | **9:10am** ✓ | **312** |
| Sun 8 — rain plan carousel | 6:30pm | **posted Sat 7, a day early** | — |

So the real split is **two evenings against three mornings**, not three and
three, and three of the six landed in the other arm.

**On the numbers as they stand, mornings are ahead**: 216, 165, 189 and 312
(mean 221) against 130 and 173 (mean 152). Saturday's walkie-talkie image is
the best single post of the week at 312, and it is a morning one.

Two things make that stronger rather than weaker, and one makes it weaker:

- The two evening posts are the **oldest** of the six and have had the most
  time to accumulate, and they are still the lowest two.
- The two best posts of the week are both **planner-life/planner-feelings
  images**, not demos: the walkie-talkie list at 312 and the "best event"
  image at 346 and 309 across its two copies. That is the format she added on
  Nov 4 on the strength of another account's sends, and on week one it is
  carrying.
- The morning arm contains the launch Reel, which carries a CTA and a product
  link — not a format advantage, if anything a drag on watch-through.
- But three of six are in the wrong arm **by accident**, so this is three
  against two by circumstance, not by design. Views on this account have run
  124–317 over the same fortnight for reasons nothing here controls.

**And Sunday's went up on Saturday.** She posted the rain-plan carousel the
day it was approved rather than in its Sun 8 slot, so Sat 7 carries two
TikToks and Sun 8 none. That is the fourth of seven pieces to land somewhere
other than its slot, and it points at the real cause: the slots live in this
repo and in the guide, not in her TikTok scheduler, so they are being matched
by memory at the moment she uploads.

**What to tell her at the recap:** the test did not run as designed, mornings
are ahead on what did run, and two against three is too few to settle it. The
honest options are to call it for mornings on a weak signal, or to run week 2
properly with the slots actually set in the TikTok scheduler at the time the
plan names.

Her own instruction stands either way: **she decides at the Sun 8 recap.**
