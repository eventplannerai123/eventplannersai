### DETAILS

**Hook.** Payoff first: the finished one-page caterer sheet, readable in the
first 2 seconds. Overlay, first 2 seconds: **"142 RSVPs. 23 dietary notes,
written 14 different ways."**

**The file.** `Holiday Party RSVP List`, a 180-person corporate holiday party.
One row per invitee: name, company department, RSVP status, meal choice,
dietary notes (free text), plus-one name, plus-one meal choice.

- 180 invited: **142 attending, 31 declined, 7 no reply**.
- Meal choice among the 142: **Beef 54, Chicken 48, Fish 22, Vegetarian 18**.
- **23 guests wrote a dietary note**, in 14 different wordings — "no gluten",
  "celiac", "GF please", "gluten free pls" and so on — which collapse to six
  real restrictions: **gluten-free 8, nut allergy 5, dairy-free 4, shellfish
  allergy 3, vegan 2, pescatarian 1**.
- **Three rows contradict themselves**: two chose Beef and wrote "no red meat"
  and "vegetarian", one chose Chicken and wrote "vegan".
- **Six plus-ones have no meal choice at all**, so the caterer's real number is
  not the column total.

**Prompt to paste** (upload the sheet first): *Here's the RSVP list for our
180-person holiday party. Give me what the caterer needs: the meal count for
each option, every dietary restriction grouped and counted with the guest names
against each, and flag anyone whose meal choice contradicts their dietary note
or who hasn't chosen a meal yet.*

**What it should catch.** 54 / 48 / 22 / 18 adding to 142; 23 free-text notes
grouped into six restrictions with the right counts; the three contradictions
named; and the six plus-ones with no meal choice called out as unknowns rather
than folded into a total. **Wrong if** it reports 142 meals without saying six
of them are not chosen yet, or if it silently reassigns the three
contradictions instead of flagging them.

**Voiceover script** (CC, ElevenLabs, Eryn, about 40 words): *"A hundred and
forty-two yeses, and twenty-three dietary notes written twenty-three different
ways. I upload the RSVP list and ask for what the caterer actually needs. Six
restrictions, counted, with names. And three people whose meal contradicts
their own note."*

**Takeaway overlay** (last 3 seconds): **"Send the caterer the conflicts, not
just the counts."**

**Caption to paste** (Instagram and TikTok):

>
> Gluten-free, GF, celiac, "no gluten please" — four spellings of one table.
> The caterer needs them grouped and counted, with names, not a column of free
> text. It found six real restrictions in the twenty-three notes.
>
> The part I'd have missed: three people chose a meal that contradicts their
> own note, and six plus-ones never chose at all. So the real number to send
> isn't 142 — it's 142 with nine asterisks.

>
