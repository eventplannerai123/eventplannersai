## Stories and trials cannot be checked from here

In the same message she said "the instagram piece has been posted". The feed
was read back and its most recent post is still the Oct 5 hidden-fees carousel,
so it was not a feed post. Sat 10's plan has no Instagram Reel at all — only the
then-vs-now **Story** with the receipts poll — so that is what she meant.

Worth writing down because it will recur: **Stories do not appear in
`INSTAGRAM_GET_IG_USER_MEDIA`.** A Story expires in 24 hours, so her word is
the only record there is, and the absence of a feed post is not evidence that
nothing went out.

**The half of this that said Trial Reels are also missing was wrong, and was
corrected on 2026-10-15.** It was extrapolated from the missing Story rather
than observed. Trials *do* appear in the media edge — they are published, and
only their distribution is restricted. The field that actually distinguishes
them is **`is_shared_to_feed`**: `true` on every Reel posted from here, and
`false` on exactly the two planned trials. Request it explicitly; it is not in
the default field set.

That wrong line cost something: on 2026-10-15 this session used it to tell the
author two of her planned trials had gone out as ordinary Reels. They had not.

## Rules

- **SCHEDULED COUNTS AS DONE — her instruction, 2026-10-30: "if something is
  scheduled you can consider it done."** This reverses the rule below, which was
  this session's own and not hers. A piece she has scheduled in the TikTok app
  gets its guide line struck the moment she says she scheduled it, and the row
  here reads scheduled rather than confirmed, which is the honest record without
  leaving the task hanging.

  The reversed rule, kept because the reasoning is still true even though the
  conclusion is not hers: *"Scheduled is not posted. From 2026-10-10 she also
  schedules uploads in the TikTok app ahead of time. A scheduled piece gets its
  own row and stays that way until she says it went out — a schedule can be
  edited, moved or silently fail, and nothing reports back here either way."*
  It still cannot be read back, so the row stays marked as scheduled. What
  changed is that an unstruck line is no longer the way to carry that doubt.
- **Never offer a piece as "unposted" from a schedule line.** Check here, and if
  it is not confirmed, ask.
- A piece can be live before its planned date. The calendar says when something
  was intended, not when it happened.
- Two TikTok slots that the plan still shows as filled are in fact free: **Tue
  13 Oct** (held the vendor quotes cut) and whatever day the centerpieces piece
  would have taken.
