# What has actually gone up on TikTok

**Why this file exists.** TikTok uploads are the author's — nothing reports
back into this repo — so a day's schedule only ever records what was *meant* to
run. On 2026-10-04 two pieces were offered to her as Monday's TikTok on the
strength of repo notes, and both were already live: the vendor quotes cut and
the centerpieces piece. Twice in one day.

The fix she and this session agreed: **when she uploads, she sends one line, and
it is recorded here.** Nothing else is reliable.

## Status

`confirmed` means she said so in the conversation. `handed over` means the file
and caption went to her and nothing has come back — **not** that it is live, and
never a reason to treat the piece as unposted either.

back catalogue is finished: the Sep 14 floor plan, Sep 20 vendor contract and

## Rules

- **Never offer a piece as "unposted" from a schedule line.** Check here, and if
  it is not confirmed, ask.
- A piece can be live before its planned date. The calendar says when something
  was intended, not when it happened.
- Two TikTok slots that the plan still shows as filled are in fact free: **Tue
  13 Oct** (held the vendor quotes cut) and whatever day the centerpieces piece
  would have taken.
