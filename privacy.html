This policy explains what information The Event Planners AI ("we", "us", "the service") collects, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and how you can have it deleted. It covers the data we access from social platforms you connect, including TikTok.

1. Who we are

The Event Planners AI is an assistant that helps event planners prepare and publish content to their own social media accounts. The service is operated from New York, United States.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • Contact details you provide, such as your email address.
  • Content you upload or create through the service — videos, images, captions, and scheduling details.

2.2 Information from platforms you connect

When you connect a TikTok account, TikTok gives us an access token and the specific data covered by the permissions you approved. Today that means:

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic) — your open ID, display name, and avatar. We use this only to show you which account is connected and to attach your content to the correct account.
  • Content publishing (video.upload, video.publish) — permission to upload the videos you supply to your own account, as a draft or a direct post, according to your choice.

We request the narrowest set of permissions the service needs. We do not access your direct messages, your private drafts, or any account you have not explicitly connected.

2.3 Information collected automatically

We keep basic operational logs — timestamps, error messages, and records of actions taken on your behalf — so the service can be debugged and so you have a record of what was posted and when.

3. How we use information

  • To perform the actions you ask for, such as uploading a video to your TikTok account.
  • To display your connected accounts and the status of your scheduled content.
  • To diagnose problems, prevent abuse, and keep the service working.
  • To respond to you when you contact us.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your content or your TikTok data to train machine learning models. We do not use it for advertising or share it with data brokers.

4. How we share information

We share information only in these situations:

  • With the platform you are publishing to. Content you schedule is transmitted to TikTok in order to post it. TikTok's own handling of that data is governed by TikTok's privacy policy.
  • With service providers that host or operate our infrastructure, and that process data only on our instructions.
  • When legally required — in response to valid legal process, or to protect the rights and safety of users or the public.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Access tokens are kept only while your account is connected. Disconnecting the integration deletes the token.
  • Profile information from TikTok is retained while the connection is active and deleted within 30 days of disconnection.
  • Content you upload is retained until you delete it, or until 30 days after your account is closed.
  • Operational logs are retained for up to 12 months.

You can revoke our access at any time from TikTok's own settings (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions). Revoking there immediately stops our ability to act on your account.

6. Your rights

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, correction of anything inaccurate, deletion of your information, or a copy of it in a portable format. Write to anand.sahiba@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Residents of some jurisdictions, including certain U.S. states, have additional statutory rights. We honor these requests regardless of where you live.

7. Security

Access tokens and credentials are stored encrypted, transmitted over HTTPS, and restricted to the people and systems that need them. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the data.

8. Children

The service is intended for professional event planners and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International users

The service is operated from the United States. If you use it from elsewhere, your information will be processed in the United States, where privacy laws may differ from those in your country.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be communicated to connected users directly where we have a means of doing so.
