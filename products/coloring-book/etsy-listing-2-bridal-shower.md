## Description

```
A coloring station for your bridal shower — 50 original wedding pages, printed
once and spread across the table with a jar of pencils.

It is the quiet activity that actually works. Nobody has to stand up, nobody
has to guess how well they know the bride, and the people who hate games have
something to do with their hands. The pages fill up over the afternoon and the
bride keeps them.

WHAT YOU GET
• The Calm Before the Aisle — 50 original hand-drawn wedding coloring pages
• 8.5 x 11 inch PDF, single sided so nothing bleeds through
• Instant download — nothing is posted to you
• Print at home on regular paper, or send the file to any print shop

HOW TO USE IT AS A STATION
• Print 3 or 4 different pages per guest and lay them out down the middle of
  the table
• Add coloured pencils rather than markers — markers bleed through
• Ask each guest to sign the page they finish. They become the shower's
  keepsake instead of a card nobody reads twice

PAGES INCLUDE
The dress, the bouquet, the ring box, the cake, the guest book, the table
setting, the getaway car, the seating chart, the first dance — and the
details nobody draws, like the shoes under the chair at the end of the night.

PRINTING LICENCE
You may print these pages as many times as you need for the guests at ONE
party — your shower, your bachelorette, your hen weekend. Personal use only:
the file itself may not be resold, shared or distributed.

ALSO WORKS FOR
Bachelorette weekends and hen parties, a bridesmaid proposal box, a quiet
corner at the rehearsal dinner, or a gift for the bride who has been staring
at a seating chart since March.

Because this is an instant download, all sales are final — but message me if
anything at all is wrong with the file and I will sort it out.
```
